# IronIQ Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

IronIQ is a workout tracking app.

## Information We Collect

IronIQ may collect account information provided through Sign in with Apple or Google, such as your email address or account identifier.

IronIQ stores workout-related data you create in the app, including workout templates, exercises, sets, reps, duration, weight, workout history, and related session information.

If you use the Apple Watch app or HealthKit-related features, IronIQ may access workout and heart rate data only with your permission.

## How We Use Information

We use your information to provide core app functionality, including account access, workout tracking, workout history, Apple Watch workout features, and syncing workout data.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising or tracking.

We may use third-party services such as Apple, Google, or backend infrastructure providers to operate the app.

## Health Data

Health and workout data is used only to provide workout tracking features. IronIQ does not use HealthKit data for advertising or sell HealthKit data.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Workout and account data may be retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.
